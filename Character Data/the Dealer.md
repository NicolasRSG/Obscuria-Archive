The Dealer is the mischievous, opportunistic antagonist to the first cycle of the game; a dark, tall, eldritch cloaked figure, adorned with a big hat, that obscures most features of their face. Eyes and mouth are ideally highlighted in a cartoon-ish way, so that their expressions are not entirely concealed.

A trickster like ancient spirit, bound to [['the Hub']] by a curse ==🟣(needs more insight on this specific curse)==, the Dealer is an avid collector of all sorts of items and antiques, and uses the very same ==🟣Hub== as an inn/wares shop, striking deals with all sorts of passing adventurers that venture into the depths of [['the Field']].

Because of this curse, they naturally lack combat prowess, and their magick capabilities are vastly limited (but still, pretty dangerous).
