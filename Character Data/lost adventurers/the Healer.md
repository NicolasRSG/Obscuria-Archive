The healer is a druid-like supporter character who draws power from her natural surroundings to aid and empower other party members. Her abilities and spells include manipulation of natural elements, that intrinsically work along her emotional state, and for this, she is one, if not the mightiest of her party. The (soft) ==🟣animal/beast/combined==¹ aspects of her design are essential to link her role to herself, as other fae folk draw aspects from other living beings too.

She is a gentle, yet righteous fae, oath-sworn to protect all things living, and maintain close watch to the balance of nature. Thus, when the [[interplanar ink]] tainted the soil of the land, the fae folk were startled, as the foul corruption took over nature at a disturbing rate.
As their protectors, the fae folk took action immediately, sending expedition groups all across the star to suppress this dark plague in what way they could. The Healer was one of them, tho at some point in the journey she got separated from her party members, eventually being found and offered a spot in the party of [[lost adventurers]].
