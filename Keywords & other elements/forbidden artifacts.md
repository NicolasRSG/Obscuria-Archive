These are special types of items obtained during the early second cycle of the game. These forbidden artifacts are part of [[the Dealer]]'s vast, otherwordly collection of items, scattered across [['the Field']]
These artifacts may be copied by using the [[Scroll of duplicity]].
Main route artifacts: 

- "[[Frail Harrowed Cane of the Unrested]]" (Necromancy)
- "[[Fullmetal Heartcore]]" (Magicked Mechanica)
- "[[Bewitched Sundew Chimera]]" (Forbidden Biomancy)

Secret route artifacts:

- "[[Advanced Tome of Abyssal Summons]]" (Demon Summoning)
- "[[Strange Specimen]]" (Interplanar Assimilation)
