Occasionally during gameplay (or with the usage of an item to guarantee their appearance) the player may stumble upon various sorts of lost souls in the [["the Field"]], each one unique and following their own individual quests.

These lonely adventurers are only 'playable' during that specified encounter and only if accepted the help prompt, as the pc demonstrates an enduring resistance/avoidance to teamwork during their ventures.

They later prove to be essential figures that revolve around one of the game's [[endings]], assuming the player accepted/interacted (with) the major part of help/conversations.
# List of wandering adventurers¹

- [[the Celestial]] as a healer and protector (winged, paladin/cleric, powers of the divine, celestial entity);
- [[the Sage]] as the spell-caster, intellectual and well versed in ancient history/biomancy;
- [[the Marauder]] as the sheer, monstrous strength, berserker;

¹Initially, (or rather, for the majority of the game's extent) these characters are lone wanderers, preoccupied with their own, individual "questlines", they may occasionally bump into the player during ventures and offer a helping hand in combat, tho the party itself is only really established later on, off-screen, during [[ending IV]].
