Occasionally during gameplay (or with the usage of an item to guaranteed their appearance) you may stumble upon various sorts of lost souls in the [['the Field']], each one unique and following their own personal quests.

These lonely adventurers are only 'playable' during that encounter, as the PC strongly refuses to stick together during their ventures.

These few characters, even with their rare appearances, later prove to be important characters to one of the secret, good endings of the game, as they turn out to be valuable, newfound companions.
