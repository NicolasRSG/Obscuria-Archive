A dark, ink-like, corrupting 'substance' found in the innards of a [[interplanar beast]], after it got stranded in this very same dimension, circumstances unknown (various found clues point that the creature was most likely being 'hunted' by a major predator, trying to escape from something dangerous).

While common-folk were pretty much disturbed and terrified of this alien visit, even tho life on the creature was long gone, sages and scholars from across the land were deeply committed to press on with further studies of the cryptic life form, during one moment after they stumbled upon the creature's ink-sack, in which, the studious assumed the substance there present had the 'almost perfect' properties to conceive what they titled '[[magicked ink]]'

The "almost" proved itself to be painfully true when, in the attempt to recklessly make use of this power from beyond, the experiment took a turn for the worst, as it's ungovernable nature started to spread like a plague.
