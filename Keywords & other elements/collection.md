(AKA [[the Dealer]]'s collection, or "the collection") is the vast, almost infinite amount of treasures and magic artifacts they collected for thousands and thousands of years. 
The endless variety of items makes so adventurers of every kind have something that will be proven useful on their personal quests, often finding out about the existence of such items in tales and legends, or blatantly assuming a specific item could not be anywhere else, which ends up being true a surprising amount of times. 
Be it to resurrect lost party members (very convenient), acquire a comically large weapon, a spell that pays off your pending bills, or even a simple magic that boils water, there always seems to exist the type of item in that collection for every possible conceivable scenario/utility.
