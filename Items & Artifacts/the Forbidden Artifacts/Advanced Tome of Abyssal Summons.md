Forbidden Artifact of the School of 'Abyssal Magick'

Usage: Resurrects party member by offering their vessel to the mercy of the abyss, they 'come back' as an Overworld Fiend.

Item description: *"Demonic summoning ritual, said to be employed only by the mightiest of abyssal mages. This tome describes a bizarre type ritual in concerning amounts of detail, one that forfeits the lifeless body of an intelligent creature so that a demon of equal or greater power possesses them. It's explicitly mentioned that any summoner devoid of previous abyssal knowledge may suffer from immolation, mind burn, extreme dehydration (...) in an attempt to perform this ritual."*
