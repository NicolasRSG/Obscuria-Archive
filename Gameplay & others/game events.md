1. The death of one or more of the [[wandering adventurers]] during ventures on [["the Field"]]. Locks [[ending IV]];

2. A follow up to the previous event, choosing to resurrect one or more of the [[wandering adventurers]], by using an acquired [[forbidden artifact(s)]]. This still keeps [[ending IV]] locked, but prompts other different endings. NPCs will always present "concerning thoughts" upon the ressurection of a fallen party member using any forbidden artifact.

3. Death of one or more of the [[wandering adventurers]] BY [[the Dealer]]; Prompts possibility of resurrecting such member(s), locking [[ending IV]] if so.

4. If prompted, refusing the party invitation that triggers in [[ending IV]] ( because maybe you just so happen to hate everyone and would rather romanticize your loneliness), so uhh fine, I guess (???).

5. Forced liberation, player finds a way to break their own curse of immortality, by using a specific type of artifact from [[the Dealer]]'s collection ==🟣(to be designed)==¹, locking all other [[endings]] and immediately ending that run/save.
