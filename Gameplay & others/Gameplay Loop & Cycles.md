Cycle 1 - *"To hunt the accursed beasts"*

The PC roams into the 'hub'. After the initial interaction with the Dealer, they are bound by this 'contract', thus forced to do the entity's bidding until they release you.

The specifics of the deal then, tell you that you're to slay the accursed beasts that roam outside the hub ([['the Field']]), collect their so called power, and bring them back to the Dealer, you're permitted to pick them any order you'd like, BUT you can only pick the next one after the previous one is complete.
After picking your mark, buy items and artifacts of your choice and proceed to the next phase.
The first cycle is complete after all 3 beasts are slain and their routes resolved. ==🟣(write on 'route resolution')==

Cycle 2 - *"To bring back the party"*

After hunting all objectives, the Dealer claims the souls of the ones you brought them, gaining immeasurable power, managing to release themselves from the ghostly shackles of the 'hub'. During this event, the PC hears the desperate calls for aid, in ghostly, familiar tones, thus rapidly compelled to act, not entirely realizing why, to face the now all powerful Dealer and claim back the souls they had so easily forfeit. (Prompts boss fight, defeat the Dealer to progress)

The setting is majorly transformed after this event, thus initiating the second cycle, as the PC recognizes the lost souls as their long lost party members, and gains the newfound objective of bringing them back to life, at any cost. (write on 'routes to artifacts of resurrection')

Only after exhausting *all [[methods of resurrection]]¹* that the player is able to proceed to the final part of the game, where the cycle is concluded, based on player dialogue choice and chosen resurrection method for each specific member.

Making use of specific items/artifacts (how does one acquire them?)
the PC unlocks a method of resurrection, that may be used in one, of the three available party members corpses. Each party member can be resurrected by only ONE artifact during that run, as they are consumed upon use. This encourages the player to do other runs, using different combinations of bringing the party members 'back'.
