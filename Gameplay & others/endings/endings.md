- [[ending I]]: no party members are revived, [["player character"]] continues to roam alone;

- [[ending II]]¹: all party members are revived, using one of the three main artifacts for each;

- [[ending III]]²: all party members are revived with the same [[method(s) of resurrection]], by using three of the same chosen forbidden artifact.

-  [[ending IV]] (the good one): unlocks ==🟣after exhausting all *five* possible methods==; pc abandons the quest to resurrect their allies, cleansing their souls from the taint, and is offered to join the [[wandering adventurers]] as a party member.

¹ various possible combinations;
² during a run, the [[scroll of duplicity]] can be used on a chosen forbidden artifact, unlocking  *five* new ending variations, one for each of the available [[method(s) of resurrection]];

Some specific [[game events]] can trigger during some runs, prompting even further different/secret endings.
