Basic, generic medieval fantasy setting but with its own twists.
The outside world, in places such as [["the Field"]], is partially afflicted by [[interplanar ink]], but other than that, other aspects of the world itself are unimportant to the narrative.

This selfsame dark substance is responsible for the death and corruption of your beloved party members, their souls still bound to the land, as their now tainted souls seem unable to move on, even after their death.
